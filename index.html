Overview

Built to inspect where larger systems cannot.

The platform combines compact mechanical design with intelligent sensing and wireless operation. Its mecanum wheel drive makes it highly maneuverable, while onboard sensing and AI-assisted imaging support safer, more informed inspections in constrained or hard-to-access environments.
